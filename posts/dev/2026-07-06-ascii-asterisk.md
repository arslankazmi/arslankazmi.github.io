A live 3D rendering of a Claude-asterisk-inspired starburst — 15 to 30 thin cylindrical shafts, shot out from the center in random directions across all three axes and pulsing to random lengths independently, a fresh layout every time this page loads — drawn entirely in text characters. No canvas, no WebGL, no Three.js, just plain JavaScript rewriting a `<pre>` tag.
