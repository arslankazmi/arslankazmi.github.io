---
title: On reading slowly, on purpose
date: 2026-05-20
tags: [notebook]
blurb: Why I stopped using a highlighter, and what I do instead.
read: 4 min
---

I used to read with a highlighter, and what I ended up with was a book full of someone
else's good sentences — bright yellow proof that I'd *been* there, evidence with no
memory attached.

Now I read slowly, on purpose, with a pencil and the back of the last page. When a
sentence stops me, I don't mark it. I close the book and try to say it back in my own
words. If I can't, I didn't really read it; I just looked at it.

It's slower. That's the point. The goal was never to finish more books. The goal was to
keep more of the ones I finish.
