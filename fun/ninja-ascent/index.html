How to climb
You run up one wall at a time. Jump to wall-jump across the gap. In the air you're safe from things on the walls, but not from what's falling.
The sword
Slash cuts anything wooden: planks, signs, crates and boxes. It does nothing to spikes, pipes, bricks or metal blocks: those you dodge.
